This project helps you monitor jaw muscle activity and provides biofeedback (via warning beeps or alarms) to interrupt bruxism events.
Below you'll find everything you need to build and configure your device.

A full assembly of the modular device featuring the core module, wall mount, phone mount, and battery module.

🚨 SAFETY NOTICE
To reduce risk of electrocution, NEVER connect electrodes to your body when the circuit is connected to mains power in any way. Use a battery-only powered setup.

This is not a medical device, responsibility for its usage is entirely yours.
