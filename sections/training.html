☀️ Training the reflex

Training a relaxing reflex is crucial to avoid being woken up by the device all the time.

0. The Classical Conditioning Principle

For the full details, a dedicated Wikipedia article is available.

Conditioning occurs when pairing a behaviour with some kind of stimulus.

  • Think about dog training: you say a command immediately when they do some action, then reward them with a treat to associate command|action.
  • Repeat this enough times and eventually the dog will do the action when hearing the command, even without a constant reward.
  • In our case, the goal is to train our body to relax the jaw when it hears a particular tone (the command).

We do this through the trainer feature of the Android application.

1. Training using the application

  • Open the app. The "Start trainer" button is only shown when disabling the "Autostart Trainer" switch.
  • Turn it off, then tap the button, then turn it back on.
  • This way you will train for the day and it will automatically start the next day when tracking ends.
  • Training procedure: throughout the day it will randomly play the same tone as the Arduino device.
  • This plays through the "Alarm" volume channel, so make sure that alarms are not muted. This is different from the "media" and "ringtone" volume channels, so you can use your smartphone as usual.
  • Relax your jaw immediately when you hear it.

Once this has become a habit, you might start responding to the beeping even while asleep. This is the part that supposedly interrupts bruxism events without waking you up.

2. What if the tone does not work for me?

In all cases, the device will start an alarm and wake you up if you don't respond.

In case of hearing difficulties, at this point in time you need to edit the app and device code to change the tone to a frequency that works for you. (Current tone frequency: 2600 Hz)
