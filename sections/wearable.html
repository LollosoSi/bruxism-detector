⌚ Wearable Devices & Sleep Trackers

You can match your bruxism data with sleep data from your sleep tracker to get a better overview of your night sessions.

Graph Integration

  • If available, your sleep data (sleep phases, heart rate and SpO2 + stress for Mi Band users) will be included in your final graph.
  • To do this manually with the desktop program, you will need to export the tracker data to a compatible format first. Currently only done through the Android app.

Health Connect (All recent Android devices)

This is the currently most compatible and recommended method.

If you are using any wearable which syncs with this system service, configure syncing.

  • In the detector app, tap "Smartband" and allow permissions to Health Connect
  • You don't need to repeat this step. The next time it will sync automatically before generating new Graphs.

Mi Band Support

The Android App has a specific integration for Mi Band users.

Root method (Mi Fitness):

  • Tap Smartband in the app. Do not consent Health Connect. Consent Root Access.
  • Requirements: To use this feature, you must have either a rooted Android device or manually provide the database (fitness_data) file.

Nonroot method (Gadgetbridge):

  • From the Gadgetbridge app, export the database to Documents/RECORDINGS/Gadgetbridge/Gadgetbridge.db
    • You can set this to "automatic" and to a time when you're awake.
  • Now go to the detector app. Tap Smartband. Do not consent Health Connect. You'll be asked to select the db file as a fallback method.
  • After importing is completed, tap "Graphs"
  • The app does not import the database automatically before generating the graphs, and does not update older ones. Only the most recent session gets regenerated.
  • With this procedure, you also export the "Stress" parameter from the smartband. If you don't need this, then configure Gadgetbridge to export automatically to Health Connect.

Recommended: Configuring Gadgetbridge to Health Connect:

  • Go to App Settings > External Integrations > Health Connect
  • Enable Health Connect Export
  • Also enable it to sync automatically
  • Now the app will also automatically include everything uploaded in Health Connect before generating your graph!
    • The only downside of this method is that you won't see "Stress" values, but you will get automatic syncing.
